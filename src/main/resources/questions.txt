Q: A train travels 120 km in 2 hours. Find its speed in km/h.
A: 40
B: 50
C: 60
D: 70
Correct: C
Explanation: Speed = Distance / Time = 120 km / 2 hours = 60 km/h.

Q: Look at the series: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, ... What number should come next?
A: 48
B: 56
C: 64
D: 72
Correct: C
Explanation: Each number is multiplied by 2 to get the next number: 32 * 2 = 64.
